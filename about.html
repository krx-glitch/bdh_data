Our Programs

Explore the diverse dance, yoga, and fitness programs we offer, tailored for every age and skill level.

MINI MOVERS – Kids Dance Program

Age Group: 3–14 Years

“Dance, Fun & Confidence for Kids”

Designed specially for children aged 3–14 years. Introduces kids to Hip‑Hop and Bollywood dance styles.

  • Discipline
  • Teamwork
  • Creative expression
  • Confidence building

Learning Structure: Warm‑up sessions, simple dance steps, small routines, practice sessions, cool‑down activities.

Skills Developed: Rhythm, Balance, Coordination, Confidence

START TO DANCE (STD) – Beginner Program

Age Group: 15+ to Adults

“Rhythm, Energy & Confidence – For Beginners”

Foundation program for beginners covering Basic Hip‑Hop, Bollywood and trending dance styles.

  • Step‑by‑step guidance
  • Body movement and rhythm understanding
  • Confidence building
  • Easy‑to‑follow classes

Additional Benefits: Mentor feedback, small performances, personal guidance.

Completion Benefits: T‑shirt, Bottle, Notebook, Beginner certificate.

THE YOUTH VIBE LAB – Intermediate Program

Age Group: 15+ to Adults

“Upgrade Your Skills – Dance Beyond Basics”

Designed for passionate dancers ready to level up. Multi‑style training includes Hip‑Hop, Bollywood, Contemporary.

  • Dance techniques
  • Stage presence
  • Musicality
  • Energy control
  • Teamwork

Program Activities: Group practices, creative sessions, stage rehearsals, competition preparation.

Completion Benefits: T‑shirt, Bottle, Certificate.

MOMENTUM – Advanced Program

Age Group: Teen to Adult

“Push Your Limits – Dance Like a Pro”

Advanced‑level program for dancers aiming for professional performance.

Dance Styles Included: Hip‑Hop, Jazz, Contemporary, Bollywood, Fusion.

  • Warm‑ups
  • Advanced routines
  • Freestyle challenges
  • Stage rehearsals
  • Audition preparation
  • Big performance preparation
  • Professional development

Students receive: Certifications, Portfolio shoots, Workshops with top artists.

PROFESSIONAL DANCE TRAINING PROGRAM

Age Group: 16 Years & Above

“Step Up to a Professional Stage”

Professional‑level training lasting 6–12 months, covering Hip‑Hop, Bollywood, Jazz, Contemporary.

  • Choreography composition
  • Stage performance
  • Audition preparation
  • Teaching skills
  • Leadership development
  • Career preparation

Designed to help students become professional dancers, instructors, or performers.

Participants receive: Certification, Professional portfolio shoot, Performance opportunities at major events and live shows.

Artist Collaboration & Workshop Policy

Tagline: “Learn from the Best, Grow with the Rest”

  • Respect: Respect artists, their time, and creative process.
  • Punctuality: Be on time for workshops and rehearsals; late entry may be restricted.
  • Focus & Discipline: Stay attentive, avoid distractions, and follow instructions carefully.
  • Photos & Videos: No photography or videography without prior permission from BDH or the artist.
  • Positive Environment: Encourage fellow participants and maintain a supportive atmosphere.
  • Safety First: Follow safety guidelines and inform the team of any concerns.

Workshops & Artist Collaboration

  • Weekly workshops with city‑level artists, each lasting ~2 hours, included in advanced training programs.
  • Special events: Celebrity workshops every 3 months for exposure to top performers.
