How to Get Here

Navigating your way to Bhopal's premier dance destination is easy with these local tips.

By Car / Taxi

Located just off the main Rachna Nagar road. Free parking is available directly in front of the studio complex.

Public Transport

The nearest bus stop is 10 Number Market. From there, it's a brisk 5-minute walk or a quick auto-rickshaw ride.
